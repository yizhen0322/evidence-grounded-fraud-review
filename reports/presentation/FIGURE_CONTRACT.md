# CP2 presentation visual contract

## Core conclusion

The project contribution is a measurable, fail-closed boundary between model evidence and optional local-LLM articulation. The fraud detector and SHAP produce the evidence; the LLM may restate only a minimised package; deterministic validation and fallback decide what reaches the analyst.

## Evidence chain

Each visual must carry one distinct part of the argument:

1. Problem: a risk score and SHAP attribution do not automatically produce a safe analyst brief.
2. Research position: ULB is the supporting real-data detector benchmark, while S0 is the readable semantic and operational evaluation context.
3. Method: immutable evidence flows through reason codes, optional local generation, validation, and deterministic fallback.
4. Detector evidence: six groups and five seeds show a mixed result rather than a single winning configuration.
5. Narrative evidence: strict and simple prompts produce different detected violation and fallback rates under one fixed contract.
6. Operational evidence: the workbench presents the same source-labelled artifacts without allowing workflow writes to modify research evidence.
7. Human evidence: the interim pilot separates perceived clarity, trust, and evidence comprehension.

Repeated full-size visuals are not acceptable unless the second use reveals a different crop or annotated conclusion.

## Archetype and hierarchy

- Archetype: schematic-led scientific presentation with quantitative validation slides.
- Hero visual: the evidence-to-delivery architecture appears once at readable scale.
- Supporting visuals: a detector-group matrix, metric bars, guardrail outcome bars, a readable case-review comparison, human-evaluation charts, and a final claim-boundary summary.
- Palette: restrained navy, teal, neutral grey, and one warning accent. Red and green must not be the only encodings.

## Backend and export

- Backend: Python and `python-pptx` for all generated slide graphics and layout.
- Canvas: 16:9, 13.333 by 7.5 inches.
- Projection rule: body text should remain readable from the back of a classroom; nested screenshots must be cropped or replaced with simplified panels.
- Export bundle: source Markdown, matching speaker script, PPTX, PDF, and rendered slide PNGs.

## Reviewer risks

- The work may appear to contain separate detector, synthetic-data, LLM, dashboard, and human-study topics unless every slide returns to the same evidence boundary.
- Repeated architecture and result images make the deck look unfinished.
- Small screenshot text can make technically correct material unusable during projection.
- Human and environmental statements must remain bounded: the study did not measure professional analyst benefit, production-bank outcomes, energy use, or carbon impact.
